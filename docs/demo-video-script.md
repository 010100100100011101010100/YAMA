# The camera that never arrived

Use this story for the recording. It introduces a new seller name and a new loss amount, then connects to four existing reports through one phone number. The audience can verify the connection and the different reported outcomes on screen.

## Opening line for the video

“These reports use made-up people and incidents. I’m going to report a new incident under a different seller name and see whether Yama can find a connection from the contact details.”

## 1. Tell Saathi what happened

Start a new chat in English. Paste or speak the following account. For a text recording, pasting keeps the phone number exact.

> My father is retiring next week, and I wanted a camera to record the celebration. A seller calling himself Armaan Rane offered me a used mirrorless camera through Copper Finch. Three days ago, I paid ₹12,500 in advance by UPI. He promised next-day dispatch.
>
> His contact number is +1 202 555 0101.
>
> After I paid, he asked for another ₹1,500 to release the courier parcel. I did not pay that extra amount. The tracking page has stayed at “label created” for three days. I asked for a fresh video of the camera with that day’s date, but he resent the same old clip and then stopped answering.
>
> I still have the invoice, payment screenshot and chat. I have not contacted my bank yet. I do not know whether Armaan Rane is his real name. Can you help me understand what to do?
<!-- DEMO-STORY-END -->

If Saathi asks a clarification, answer consistently with the account: ₹12,500 paid, ₹1,500 requested but unpaid, UPI, three days ago, no bank contact yet. Do not invent a transaction reference, complaint number or evidence upload. Saying “I have a screenshot” does not attach it.

Open **Your notes**, check the amount and contact, acknowledge the account and press **Confirm my notes**. This is the user's confirmation, not independent verification.

## 2. Show the handoff and contact match

Choose **Ask a question**, then send this without an @ prefix:

> Drishti, check whether this phone number appears in other saved reports. Summarise what happened in each report and what was recovered. My seller used the name Armaan Rane. Does that affect the contact match? Keep the reporters’ identities private.

A plain agent name routes through Saathi, who passes the turn to @Drishti. Show the typing state and handoff. The exact reply wording can vary; verify the facts below.

## 4. Ask a useful follow-up

> @Drishti, compare the laptop report with the camera report. What evidence and follow-up actions were recorded in each? Which differences are known, and what can’t we tell about why one person recovered money and the other didn’t?

The reply should distinguish recorded actions from explanations we cannot establish. A successful refund in another report must not become a promise about yours. **Your notes should stay confirmed** because this is a question, not a correction.

## 5. Bring in Disha and public sources

> @Disha, I’m in India and paid ₹12,500 by UPI. Search for official consumer-reporting sources and read the relevant page. Give me three practical next steps, with clickable sources. Separate the published guidance from what happened in the saved reports. Don’t submit anything for me.

Show a working source hyperlink/preview and any useful question card. On a successful lookup, the server uses a generic topic search through Exa and reads an authorised official page through Firecrawl. If the lookup fails, show the real failure; do not narrate it as a successful search. Guidance and source wording can change.

## Recording order

Aim for 3–4 minutes of edited footage: opening/account, notes confirmation, handoff, four-report comparison, one privacy/media close-up, follow-up, then Disha's sourced next steps. Trim provider waiting time while retaining a short typing/handoff moment; response latency is not a fixed part of the script.
